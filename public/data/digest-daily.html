The money question
 

What we would actually stake

A probability is half an answer. The builder takes today's card, prices every call against the odds you are actually offered, and spreads a budget so the expected return covers what you put in. Not match by match, but across the plan as a whole: a short price can ride along if the rest of the card carries it.

Fair warning, it is a killjoy. It will usually tell you to stake less than you hoped, and some days it will tell you to stake nothing at all. That is the feature, not a bug.
